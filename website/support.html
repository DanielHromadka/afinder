Common questions

The Mac App Store version cannot open a folder

The App Store version runs in the macOS sandbox and sees only what you allow. Open Settings → General → Disk access and add the folder or disk (choose Macintosh HD for the whole disk). External and network disks are added separately.

How do I connect my phone?

In aFinder open Settings → Phone, turn the companion on and choose Pair a phone. In aFinder Remote tap Pair a computer and scan the QR code, then check that both devices show the same code.

The phone says the computer is offline

aFinder must be running on the computer with the companion turned on, and both devices need an internet connection. They do not have to be on the same network.

Viewing the computer's screen stays black or ends after a while

The computer has to allow screen recording for aFinder (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording). When no direct connection is possible the picture goes through a relay at lower quality for up to 10 minutes, then rests for 30 minutes.

Where are my settings?

On your computer only: in aFinder's application data folder. Uninstalling the app and deleting that folder removes everything.
